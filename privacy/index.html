隐私条款Privacy Policy

Tylina 是 OrangeX4 开发的专有桌面软件。本条款说明应用读取、保存和联网的范围。Tylina is proprietary desktop software developed by OrangeX4. This policy explains what the application reads, stores, and accesses online.

简要说明In short

无需账号；文档不会上传到 Tylina 服务；0.1.0 不包含广告、使用情况统计或自动上传的崩溃报告。No account is required; documents are not uploaded to a Tylina service; version 0.1.0 has no ads, usage analytics, or automatic crash-report uploads.

文档与应用数据

Tylina 不要求注册账号，也不会把你的文档或工作区文件上传到 Tylina 服务。

文件和文件夹保留在你打开的位置。草稿、偏好设置、最近项目、本地版本历史与模板设置保存在设备上的 Tylina 应用数据目录。

网络访问

以下情况可能使用网络：

  • 模板库从 packages.typst.org 读取公开的包信息与缩略图。
  • Typst 与 Tinymist 可能下载文档所使用的包。
  • “导入内容”可能下载你所选内容中引用的公开 HTTP 或 HTTPS 图像。
  • 开始性能分析后，可以在 Perfetto 中打开 Tinymist trace。
  • “网站”“反馈”和文档中的链接会在默认浏览器中打开。

发送给第三方服务的请求适用该服务自己的隐私政策。

文件、导出与终端

Tylina 会读取你选择打开或导入的文件与文件夹。导出内容只会写入你选择的位置。

集成终端会在工作区文件夹中运行本地 shell。你在终端中输入的命令可能读取文件、启动程序或访问网络，这些行为不受 Tylina 控制。

反馈

“反馈”菜单会在浏览器中打开 GitHub issue 表单。除非你有意分享，否则请勿附上机密文档、密码、个人信息或私有项目内容。通过 GitHub 提交的信息按 GitHub 隐私声明处理。

删除数据

从“最近项目”中移除一项，不会删除原文件或文件夹。删除草稿会从 Tylina 中移除该草稿。关闭 Tylina 后，删除其应用数据目录，可以清除偏好设置、草稿恢复数据与本地版本历史。

变更与联系

Tylina 发生变化时，本条款可能随之更新。隐私相关问题可以通过 Tylina 反馈页面提交。

Documents and application data

Tylina does not require an account. It does not upload your documents or workspace files to a Tylina service.

Files and folders stay in the locations you open. Drafts, preferences, recent items, local version history, and template settings are stored in Tylina's application-data folder on your device.

Network access

Tylina may use the network in the following situations:

  • Template Gallery loads public package information and thumbnails from packages.typst.org.
  • Typst and Tinymist may download a package used by your document.
  • Import Content may download an image from a public HTTP or HTTPS address found in content you choose to import.
  • Profiling may open a Tinymist trace in Perfetto after you start profiling.
  • Website, Feedback, and links in documents open in your default browser.

Requests sent to third-party services are covered by the privacy policy of that service.

Files, export, and terminal

Tylina reads files and folders that you choose to open or import. Export writes to a destination you choose.

The integrated terminal runs your local shell in the workspace folder. Commands entered in the terminal may read files, start programs, or access the network. Their behavior is outside Tylina's control.

Feedback

The Feedback menu opens a GitHub issue form in your browser. Do not attach confidential documents, passwords, personal information, or private project material unless you intend to share it. Information sent through GitHub is handled under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Removing data

Removing an item from Recents does not delete the original file or folder. Deleting a draft removes that draft from Tylina. You may remove preferences, draft recovery data, and local version history by closing Tylina and deleting its application-data folder.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Tylina changes. Privacy questions can be submitted through the Tylina feedback page.
